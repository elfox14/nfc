Frequently Asked Questions About NFC for Real Estate

How does an NFC card help a real estate agent close deals faster?+
One tap gives the client the project location on maps, unit photos, the virtual tour, and updated price sheets — everything needed to decide, without waiting for WhatsApp files.
Can I update unit prices on the card without reprinting?+
Yes. The card links to your digital profile, updated anytime from your dashboard — new prices appear instantly for everyone holding your card.
What if the client is older and not tech-savvy?+
The experience is simpler than opening an app: just tap the card on the phone. As backup, the card carries a QR code on its back scanned by any regular camera — suitable for all ages.
Can I link the card to a 3D virtual tour of the property?+
Absolutely. Add your virtual tour or project video link to your digital profile, and clients open the tour right after tapping — perfect for out-of-town or international buyers.
What does an NFC card cost a real estate agent?+
A one-time cost equal to a tiny fraction of a single deal's commission. With reprinted brochures eliminated, most agents recover the cost on their first deal — compare NFC vs QR to pick your fit.
