Privacy Policy

Our platform respects user privacy. We retain account details, designs, saved cards, and account-related requests to provide the service until the user deletes them or deletes the account.

Google API Services Usage Disclosure

If you choose to "Sign in with Google," our application will only request access to your basic profile information (Name and Email) for the purpose of creating your account and securely authenticating your login. We use it to create and authenticate your MC PRIME account. Revoking the app in your Google Account stops future Google access, but it does not automatically delete your MC PRIME account or the data stored by us. You can delete that data from the dashboard as described below.

Data Accessed

When you sign in with Google, our application requests access only to the following basic information:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Profile picture

We do not request any additional permissions and do not access other Google data such as contacts, Gmail, or Drive files.

Data Usage

The Google data we collect is used exclusively for the following purposes:

  • Creating a user account and logging into the MC PRIME platform
  • Displaying the user's name and managing the account session
  • Enabling the user to create and manage their digital business cards

We do not sell, share, or use your account data for advertising or training AI/ML models. We do not store your Google password or Google access tokens. We store the Google account identifier, name, and email address linked to the MC PRIME account.

Data Export and Account Deletion

Signed-in users can open Dashboard → Privacy Settings to download a JSON copy of their account data or permanently delete the account. Deletion removes the user record, owned designs, saved cards, and related save requests from MongoDB and invalidates the login session. We also attempt to delete known user-image identifiers from the configured storage provider. Provider CDN copies or backups may remain until their technical retention period expires.

Cookies and Google AdSense

Our website uses cookies and similar technologies to enhance user experience, analyze traffic, and personalize content. In addition, we use advertising services such as Google AdSense to serve ads.

  • Third party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to our website or other websites on the Internet.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to our sites and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Ads Settings.
