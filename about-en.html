We Are MC PRIME

A premier platform specializing in designing smart digital business cards. We believe first impressions start with your card, and we make that impression unforgettable.

Our Story

The idea behind MC PRIME started from a simple question: In a world where everything has become digital, why are traditional paper business cards still the default for professional networking?

We launched with a clear mission — empower individuals and businesses to create smart digital business cards powered by NFC and QR Code technology, effortlessly and completely free.

Today, thousands of professionals and companies across Egypt, Saudi Arabia, the UAE, and worldwide rely on MC PRIME to design and share their smart cards with a single tap.
